# Phase 2 — Format Spec: AI money/productivity Shorts & Reels

Run 2026-09-02 via vidIQ. Cost: 15 credits (outliers, cross-platform outliers, one transcript).
Derived from 12 cross-platform outliers and 25 YouTube Shorts outliers, all from accounts
under 50K followers.

---

## ⚠️ The finding that changes the plan

**Every single outlier in this niche is a screen recording, and almost all of them show a
face.** Not one is the AI-generated b-roll + synthetic voiceover format the pipeline was
designed around.

Recorded styles across the 12 cross-platform winners:

| Style | Count |
|---|---|
| Split screen (creator + screen recording) | 6 |
| Talking head mixed with screen recording | 4 |
| Footage compilation (no face) | 1 |
| POV + screen recording | 1 |

The value being delivered is **proof that a tool does a thing** — which requires showing the
tool's actual screen. Generated video cannot fake a real product demo, and the audience for
"make money with AI" is specifically checking whether the claim is real. This is the same
reason `@larpwithgio`'s entire premise was verifying whether another creator was lying.

**Implication:** Higgsfield's video generation is the wrong tool for the body of these videos.
It stays useful for thumbnails, b-roll cutaways, and voiceover — but the core asset is a screen
recording, which costs zero credits and needs no generation at all.

## Spec

### Duration
**45–60 seconds.** TikTok winners measured 59s, 55s, 52s, 50s, 47s. YouTube Shorts winners in
the money angle ran 31–66s. Do not make these 10–15 seconds; that length belongs to the
entertainment-AI slop cluster, which gets views but no buyers.

### Structure

1. **Hook, 0–3s — a personal stake, never a topic.** This is the single biggest lever.
   The winning hooks make a first-person claim; the losing ones announce a subject.

   | Works (real outliers) | Views |
   |---|---|
   | "I set out to see if Uncle was lying" | 255.1K |
   | "Claude erased my entire digital identity 😲" | 223.5K |
   | "how to make $1000 a DAY selling websites made in minutes 😭🙏" | 142.2K |
   | "Day 2 of trying RANDOM high-value skills until i get a job" | 104.3K |
   | "nobody's gonna believe this is a real job 😭" | 168.1K |
   | "Most teens will skip this" | 66.7K |

   Compare the best-performing *YouTube* script, which opens on the topic — "How to use ChatGPT
   and AI tools to make money online in 2026?" — and earned 26,683 views with the same subject
   matter. **Roughly 10× less reach for the same content, because the hook is a subject rather
   than a stake.**

2. **Visual: split screen from the first frame.** Creator top, screen recording bottom — or
   full-screen recording with the creator inset. Every high-multiplier outlier is showing the
   real tool doing the real thing.

3. **Body: exactly three steps.** The transcript of the top YouTube performer runs
   *first → second → third*, then one bonus. Keep it to three demonstrated steps; the demo is
   the proof.

4. **Proof beat:** show the actual output — the finished site, the generated video, the
   deleted records. Claim without artifact is what the audience is scanning for.

5. **Honesty beat.** The highest-engagement YouTube script (4.5%) explicitly says
   *"AI will not make you rich overnight. Choose one skill, practice consistently."*
   In a niche saturated with hype, the disclaimer is a differentiator, not a drag.

6. **CTA: a comment keyword.** See monetization below.

### Execution
- Pacing: **fast, heavy cuts** — every outlier was tagged "Fast" with "High" visual change.
- Captions: **dynamic burned-in captions**, not platform auto-captions.
- Audio: **voice + upbeat background music** starting on frame 1 (9 of 12 outliers).
- Effort level observed: "within a day" per video.

## Monetization — the actual mechanism

This is why the niche was chosen, and the outliers show exactly how it is done. **Neither
route waits on YouTube Partner Program or TikTok Creator Rewards thresholds.**

### 1. Comment-keyword → DM funnel

Three separate outliers run the identical play:

- `@madisondoesai` — "Comment **LOGO** and I'll send you my complete AI guide with all my
  favorite tools, prompts, and tips" · 74.9K views on 1.1K followers
- `@aiwithabhijay` — "Comment **DATA** and I'll DM you the exact prompts + setup guide!" ·
  223.5K views
- `@lauv.scale` — "Comment **MANUS** to get my full guide" · 104.3K views

The comment drives engagement (which drives reach), the DM captures the lead, and the guide
carries affiliate links. Revenue starts at view one with zero subscribers.

### 2. Tool partner programs

Sponsored tags are visible across the winners: `#manuspartner #sponsored`, `#picsartinfluencer`,
`#viewmaxpartner`, and — directly relevant — **`#higgsfieldpartner`** on `@larpwithgio`'s
255,100-view video, posted from a **572-follower** account.

Higgsfield runs a creator partner program, and this account already holds a Higgsfield Pro
subscription. Demonstrating a tool you already pay for, in a format that already works, to an
audience that already converts, is the shortest path to revenue in this plan.

## Title / caption formula

`<first-person claim or result> + <tool name> + <emoji beat>`, then hashtags mixing
tool-specific and intent tags: `#aitools #sidehustle #makingmoneyonline #facelesscontent`
plus the partner tag when applicable.

Avoid "best/top/new AI tools" phrasing entirely — Phase 1 measured those keywords down
**30–48%** year over year even as overall AI view velocity rose from 267,603 to 845,774
views/hour across the same month.

## Open decision for Phase 3

The spec requires a face and a screen recording. The approved plan assumed faceless generative
production. Three ways forward, in order of expected performance:

1. **Face + screen recording** — matches the data exactly. Highest expected reach. Requires
   appearing on camera.
2. **Screen recording + synthetic voiceover, no face** — the one no-face outlier
   (`@lamliaaxvhj`, footage compilation) still managed 85K views on 582 followers, so it is
   not disqualifying, but it discards the split-screen convention that 10 of 12 winners used.
3. **Fully generated video** — no supporting evidence in this corpus. Not recommended for this
   niche.
