# Phase 1 — Niche Selection (data sweep)

Run 2026-09-02 via vidIQ. Target: a new channel producing YouTube Shorts + TikTok/Reels,
faceless, produced with generative tooling. Cost: 35 vidIQ credits.

## Method

1. `keyword_research` mode `rising`, period `week`, no topic — what is gaining momentum overall.
2. `outliers` × 3 candidate niches — `contentType: short`, `publishedWithin: thisMonth`,
   `maxSubscribers: 50000`, sorted by breakout score. The subscriber ceiling is the point: it
   only surfaces formats that work *without* an existing audience.
3. `keyword_research` mode `research` on the two survivors — demand vs competition vs growth.

## Result 1 — Overall rising trends are news spikes, not niches

The weekly rising list was dominated by a single event: GTA 6's extended look
(8,405,889 monthly searches, +7,703%), plus Nepal news, celebrity names, and football.

These are not viable for a new channel. They decay within weeks, and the gaming variants
require game footage that cannot be legally generated or sourced at scale. **Ruled out on
production feasibility, not demand.**

One durable signal did surface here: `using claude` at 164,392 searches, +3,576% — part of a
broader AI trend confirmed below.

## Verdict

**Facts / history wins on reach.** `interesting facts` is the single best keyword measured
anywhere in this sweep: 1.16M monthly searches at competition 32.9 — an overall score of 81.2
— and growing 165%. The outlier data independently confirms sub-1,500-subscriber channels
breaking into six figures on it right now. Production is fully generative: still imagery plus
synthetic narration, no camera, no licensed footage.

**AI is the better business, not the better channel.** Higher engagement, far higher CPM, and
an obvious affiliate path — but higher competition (65 vs 32.9), a declining dominant format,
and lower reach ceiling for a new account.

## Honest caveats

1. **High views will not mean high revenue.** The facts audience skews PK/IN/VN
   (history facts top markets: US 13.6%, PK 13.6%, IN 12.2%, VN 7.5%). CPM in these markets is
   a fraction of US/UK/CA.
2. **Engagement in this niche is terrible** — 0.3–3.3%. That is precisely the failure mode of
   the existing "Bolt rant" channel: 92,102 lifetime views against 120 subscribers. Reach in
   the facts niche does not automatically convert to an audience, and the plan should treat
   subscriber conversion as a design problem from day one, not an afterthought.
3. **The niche is saturated with near-identical low-effort output.** The outlier list is full
   of channels posting the same generic fact cards. Generated visuals alone will not
   differentiate; a consistent visual signature and a distinct narration voice will.
